iHow to read this reportDefinitions, populations and limitations+

Sales & amounts

Sales use delivered orders with valid item totals. Product value adds item prices and excludes freight. Average order value divides product value by eligible orders. Amounts use source monetary units; they do not represent profit or commission revenue.

Delivery

Delivery measures need valid purchase, receipt and promised dates. Arrival on the promised calendar day is on time. Duration measures elapsed days; positive delay averages only late orders. Counts are shown beside rates.

Reviews

Each order has at most one selected valid review: latest response, then latest creation date, then review ID. Missing reviews stay outside score averages. Low scores are 1 or 2. A review can precede delivery.

Customers & filters

Customers are grouped by persistent identity. Repeat means at least two eligible orders in the selected months and buyer state, including same-day orders. Later buyers have less observation time. It is not retention or lifetime value.

Filters use purchase month and buyer location. Statuses and outcomes reflect the final source snapshot, including later deliveries and reviews. Category order counts overlap. These historical comparisons show association and do not establish causes.
