# Interview preparation

Complete these notes after finishing the analysis.

## Three-minute structure

1. Business problem and intended stakeholder.
2. Dataset and reason for using SQL.
3. One difficult data or join issue and how you solved it.
4. Two important findings with denominators and evidence.
5. Recommended action, limitations and next step.

## Practise explaining

- Why did you choose this problem?
- What is the grain of each table?
- How did you avoid double-counting across joins?
- Why did you use customer_unique_id?
- How did you handle missing dates and multiple reviews?
- Why did you choose a CTE or a window function?
- How did you verify the dashboard numbers?
- What can and cannot be concluded from the findings?
- What would you do with another month?
- Which work did you implement yourself, and how did you verify assistance?

Only describe skills and results you can demonstrate in the repository.
